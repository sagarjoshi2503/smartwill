Residuary Clause

What is a residuary clause? It's the clause that catches everything else — any asset you acquire after signing this Will, or anything you've simply forgotten to mention above. Rather than leaving that property undecided, name who should receive it here. This is compulsory.
6

Witnesses

Who can be a witness? Under the Indian Succession Act, 1925, a witness must simply be an adult (18 or older), of sound mind, and able to sign their own name.
  • You need at least two witnesses. They don't need to know what the Will says — only that you signed it in front of them.
  • It's best to avoid a witness who is also a beneficiary of this Will, or that beneficiary's spouse.
  • A witness does not need to be related to you — independent, impartial people are actually better practice, even though family members are technically allowed.

At least two witnesses are compulsory. Add more if you'd like.

Deed of Consent

What is a Deed of Consent? Because you and your spouse jointly hold property in Goa, Goan succession law requires you to formally authorise each other to each bequeath your share by way of two separate Wills — executed the same day. That mutual authorisation is recorded in this one shared Deed of Consent, signed by both of you together.
Perfectly fine for the witnesses to be identical in both documents.
